Isar Database Collections & Embedded Objects

Horizon uses an embedded fast NoSQL database (Isar) organized into 5 primary Collections and 3 Embedded sub-objects. Every collection is indexed for instant filtering, reactive live stream queries, and time-travel windowing.

Core Nutrition & Tracking Qualification Math

These mathematical rules govern target generation, frequency qualification (10% trigger rule), and routine adherence.

1. The 10% Trigger Nutrient Coverage Qualification Rule
When a food is added to the user's routine, the database evaluates its yield against the user's effective target for the 8 Trigger Nutrients:
Yield = (plannedDailyGrams / 100.0) × amountPer100g
Coverage % = (Yield / EffectiveTarget) × 100.0

IF ANY Trigger Nutrient Coverage > 10.0% → Frequency = DAILY
ELSE → Frequency = WEEKLY
8 Trigger Nutrients:
vitamin_c (Vit C) collagen (Coll.) total_fiber (Fiber) magnesium (Mg) calcium (Ca) potassium (K) creatine (Creat.) total_protein (Requires proteinIndex == 1)
2. Effective Nutrient Target Formula
Nutrient targets dynamically adjust according to the UserProfile metrics and frequency:
For Standard Nutrients: BaseTarget = rawRdaOrAi × strictness (e.g. 90%)
For Total Protein: BaseTarget = (rawRdaOrAi < 2.0 ? rawRdaOrAi × weightKg : rawRdaOrAi) × strictness

EffectiveTarget = isWeekly ? BaseTarget × 7.0 : BaseTarget
3. Windowed Synchronization (Gap Backfilling & 3-Day Lookahead)
Whenever a routine food is added, modified, or the date shifts, Horizon syncs:
1. Backfills any missing past dates between oldest record and currentDate.
2. Synchronizes 3-Day Daily Window: [Today, Today+1, Today+2].
3. Synchronizes Current Week Monday record (e.g. 2026-W36).
4. Automatically preserves any logged consumed foods (>0g) while refreshing planned placeholders.
